The Coworking Bubble

S. Ananta
Editor in Chief

Last week, three new coworking spaces opened within a single block of Canggu, each promising the same fiber internet and the same infinity pool. The island's remote-work economy has paid for a decade of runway with a single, unshakable idea—that anyone can build a company from a beach chair. That idea is now being tested by the people actually paying rent.
